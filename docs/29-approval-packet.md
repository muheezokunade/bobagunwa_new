# Approval packet for Mr Ibrahim

**Purpose:** the decisions the association needs to make so the new Bobagunwa platform can move to protected testing and then to launch. The software is built and tested; it uses the existing Namecheap hosting and adds **no new monthly or yearly subscriptions**.

Please reply against each numbered item. Where a recommendation is given, "Agree" is enough.

## A. Needed before protected staging (testing with made-up data)

1. **Permission to install the new platform on the protected test address** `v2-staging.bobagunwa.com` (made-up data only; the current website and WordPress are not touched). *Recommendation: agree.*
2. **SSL certificate for the test address** — authorise the developer to ask Namecheap support to issue the free certificate for `v2-staging.bobagunwa.com`. No purchase involved. *Recommendation: agree.*
3. **First administrator** — name the person who will hold the first administrator account (they set their own password from a one-time link).
3a. **Hosting access** — who may log in to the Namecheap account (cPanel and SSH) to install the test site? Access is needed only for the protected test address.

## B. Needed before launch

**Membership**
4. Membership number format: `BOB-000001`, `BOB-000002`, … — or another format? Numbers are permanent once issued.
5. Who may approve membership applications? (names)
6. The list of existing members (about 86 provisional names): please confirm the list and, where possible, each member's phone number, email and date of birth. Do existing members already have membership numbers?
7. How should approved applicants receive their private activation link — WhatsApp, SMS from an officer's phone, email from the association mailbox? Who checks the person's identity first?

**Money**
8. Dues and levy types and the official amounts.
9. Who records payments, and who checks (verifies) them? *Recommendation: two different people.* Should any amount above a limit need the President's approval?
10. Who may reverse a payment that was recorded in error? *Recommendation: the verifier, never the person who recorded it.*
11. When a member pays less than everything they owe, which charge should be paid first? *Recommendation: oldest first, applied by the treasurer.*
12. What happens to overpayments — kept as credit for future dues, or refunded?
13. Do you want past payments and balances brought into the system? If yes: the cut-off date and the approved records (bank statements, receipt books or a signed balance list).
14. Which reports should executives see?

**Website content**
15. Approve (or correct) the About text, values, and the Yoruba spelling "Ìwà Ọmọlúàbí".
16. The official history to publish (the draft provided earlier needs correcting before publication).
17. Leadership: names, titles, terms of office and photographs to publish, with each person's consent.
18. Contact details to publish: which email — `eweso@bobagunwa.com` or `secretariat@bobagunwa.com`? Telephone and address?

**Safety and law**
19. Backup custodians: two trusted people who will each keep the backup key safely, and where the off-site copies will be stored.
20. Legal review of the privacy notice and terms, and how long records should be kept (e.g. rejected applications, former members).

**Launch**
21. Launch approval: the date, and whether the new platform replaces the current website at `bobagunwa.com` or sits beside it on a sub-address.

**Security and procedures**
22. **Two-factor sign-in** for officers who handle money, access, settings or backups — require it after a rehearsal on the test site? *Recommendation: agree, after every such officer has enrolled.* (D-024)
23. **Integrity alert emails** — may the platform email alert notices (no financial details) from the existing association mailbox, and to whom? (D-025)
24. **Members who share a full name** — agree that an officer may add them after checking they are different people and writing down how they checked? *Recommendation: agree.* (D-026)
