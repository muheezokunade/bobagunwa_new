# Content verification checklist

Nothing below should be treated as association-approved until an authorised representative confirms it.

- [ ] Exact official name, capitalisation, Yoruba tone marks, and approved short name
- [ ] Official crest file, permitted variants, clear space, and colour values
- [ ] Meaning and approved presentation of `1980–82`
- [ ] Official founding date and history
- [ ] Motto spelling, tone marks, translation, and usage
- [ ] Values, purpose, and heritage copy
- [ ] Leadership structure, office titles, names, terms, biographies, and photographs
- [ ] Public membership eligibility and application pathway
- [ ] Whether any member directory fields may be public
- [ ] Official secretariat email (resolve `secretariat@...` vs `eweso@...`)
- [ ] Official telephone number and consent to publish it
- [ ] Official street/postal address and preferred formatting
- [ ] Approved activities, descriptions, dates, and photographs
- [ ] Approved upcoming and historical event records
- [ ] Approved news items and publication dates
- [ ] Privacy notice, data controller/contact, retention rules, and legal review
- [ ] Website terms and legal review
- [ ] Canonical production domain and social-sharing URLs
- [ ] Ownership/licensing/consent for every photograph and downloadable document
